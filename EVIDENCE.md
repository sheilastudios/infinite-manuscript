## What the visual evidence establishes

The scene trial's saved selections distinguish three states: an image generated for current prose, artwork anticipated but not displayed, and later reuse after fresh prose-based selection. Matching asset hashes support identical-byte reuse across scenes. The portrait history likewise records reuse without another image job.

This review checked the generated image files against their content identities and inspected PNG dimensions/type headers. Saved audits contain decoded transparency results. No new pixel-quality assessment, screen interaction or artistic-quality rating was performed here.

The browser records document meaningful prose/reflection surviving effects-off, past-scene views remaining separate from late current results, and restored artwork being reused. Those are retained observations, not new browser results.

## Failures, uncertainty and version limits

The earlier director-alpha accounting had eight completed entries, one invalid response and two unknown outcomes. Those remain part of that historical scope; later successes do not erase them.

An earlier portrait request was rejected. The later successful portrait concerns a different character and is recorded as a separate attempt, not a retry or alternate route around that rejection. Other earlier continuations legitimately selected no artwork. Do not describe all model turns as visual successes.

The latest reviewed browser audit is explicitly a **mechanical-flow pass with a narrative continuity finding**: a physical state changed without the necessary narrated transition. Its continuity notes did not flag that change. A narrow UI handoff fix has focused checks, but browser-level delayed-network verification remained pending.

Some currently inspected source hashes differ from the visual trial's recorded source pins. The results remain tied to their saved requests and historical versions. A hash match verifies retained bytes, not model understanding, current deployment or complete semantic correctness.
